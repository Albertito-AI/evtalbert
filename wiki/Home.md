## Foundations and future direction

EVT-Albert supports voluntary reflection on recorded sessions. Its current software stores records, calculates descriptive summaries and exports them from ordinary source files. The revised paper explains the completed source migration, the exact arithmetic and missing-data limitations, partial identifier masking, and the research needed before claiming psychological validity or benefit.

Read the **[project whitepaper](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/evtalbert/wiki/Whitepaper)** for definitions, worked examples and counterexamples, a claim-evidence map, reproduction instructions, related research and teaching questions.
